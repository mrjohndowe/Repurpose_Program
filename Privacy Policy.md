# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 11, 2026**

Repurpose Program ("Repurpose Program," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when you use the Repurpose Program website, application, and related services ("Services").

Repurpose Program allows users to connect social media accounts and create automated workflows that distribute and republish their content across supported platforms.

By using Repurpose Program, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create a Repurpose Program account, we may collect information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Username
* Profile information
* Account preferences
* Authentication information

We do not store your passwords for third-party social media platforms.

### Connected Social Media Accounts

When you connect a supported social media account, such as TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, or another supported service, we may receive information authorized by you through that platform.

Depending on the platform and permissions you approve, this may include:

* Platform user ID
* Username or display name
* Profile picture
* Channel, profile, or Page information
* Access tokens and refresh tokens
* Videos and other content you authorize us to access
* Video titles
* Captions and descriptions
* Thumbnails
* Content identifiers
* Publishing status
* Content metadata
* Engagement or performance information when authorized

Repurpose Program only requests permissions reasonably necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

## 2. User Content

When you use Repurpose Program, we may process videos, images, captions, descriptions, titles, hashtags, metadata, and other content you provide or authorize us to access.

This information is used to perform actions you request, including:

* Importing content
* Processing content
* Preparing content for another platform
* Creating publishing jobs
* Publishing or republishing content
* Scheduling content
* Maintaining automated workflows
* Tracking whether publishing succeeded or failed

You retain ownership of your content.

## 3. Workflow Information

We collect information about workflows you create in Repurpose Program.

This may include:

* Source platform
* Destination platforms
* Connected accounts
* Publishing rules
* Scheduling preferences
* Content-processing options
* Workflow status
* Publishing history
* Error information

We use this information to operate the automated publishing features you configure.

## 4. Technical and Usage Information

When you use our Services, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

* IP address

We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Repurpose Program.

We use information collected through Repurpose Program to:

* Provide and operate the Services.
* Authenticate users.
* Connect authorized social media accounts.
* Import authorized content.
* Publish content to selected platforms.
* Run automated content workflows.
* Process publishing requests.
* Maintain account connections.
* Display publishing status and history.
* Diagnose errors.
* Prevent fraud and abuse.
* Protect the security of our Services.

## 6. TikTok Information

Repurpose Program may use TikTok APIs to provide features such as TikTok account connection, authorized content access, and supported content publishing functionality.

When you connect TikTok, Repurpose Program only accesses information and permissions that you authorize through TikTok's authorization process.

TikTok information may be used to:

* Identify your connected TikTok account.
* Access content you authorize Repurpose Program to use.
* Retrieve information required to operate your workflows.
* Publish content when authorized.
* Display publishing status.

Repurpose Program does not sell TikTok user data.

Repurpose Program does not share TikTok personal information with unrelated third parties without appropriate authorization.

You may disconnect TikTok from Repurpose Program or revoke Repurpose Program's access through TikTok where supported.

## 7. YouTube and Google Information

Repurpose Program may use YouTube API Services and Google authorization services to connect your YouTube account and provide YouTube-related functionality.

Depending on the permissions you approve, Repurpose Program may access information such as:

* YouTube channel information
* Channel identifiers
* Video information
* Video metadata
* Authorized account information
* Publishing permissions
* YouTube API Data

This information is used only to provide Repurpose Program features associated with your YouTube connection.

Repurpose Program does not request or store your Google account password.

Use of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

## 9. OAuth and Access Tokens

Third-party platforms generally authorize Repurpose Program using access tokens rather than providing us with your platform password.

Access and refresh tokens may be securely stored so Repurpose Program can perform authorized actions on your behalf.

These tokens are used only for authorized platform functionality.

We take reasonable measures designed to protect tokens from unauthorized access.

If you disconnect an account, revoke authorization, or delete your Repurpose Program account, associated tokens will be deleted or invalidated as appropriate.

Repurpose Program does not control how third-party platforms process information after it has been transmitted to them.

## 14. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

If you disconnect a platform, information that is no longer necessary to provide the Services will be scheduled for deletion.

YouTube API Data associated with your account will be deleted within applicable YouTube API policy requirements after a valid deletion request or loss of authorization.

You may disconnect a social media platform from Repurpose Program.

Deleting Repurpose Program does not necessarily delete content that Repurpose Program previously published to an external platform. Content already published must generally be managed through the destination platform unless Repurpose Program provides a supported deletion feature.
